## Shared execution and client knowledge

Every backend operation carries organization/client/environment scope. Profiles, approved policy versions, source identities/versions, periods, grants, runs, occurrences, decisions, dispositions and packages remain scoped. Profiles are proposed by registration; a separately granted human approves them. Rules record proposer, approver, effective dates, evidence and supersession. Handlers receive only approved rules effective on the item's date. Proposed changes confer no authority. Alias proposals expose their normalized form in audit evidence.

Client-specific chart, entity/book, currency, vendors, receipt matching, procedures and close checklist remain separate from shared invariants. Capabilities declare required facts; missing facts block client-sample preparation. Explicit explanatory waivers are allowed only for synthetic_demo profiles and are exported. No actual Bird Bowl policy or fact is supplied by these waivers.

Each of the three workflows starts with the shared capability `evidence.inventory`, which lists the scoped sources the workflow declares and blocks with NO_SOURCE_EVIDENCE when there are none, and ends with `package.manifest`, which records the input fingerprint and scope and states that nothing was posted or signed off. Neither proves a complete ledger or statement population. Capabilities declare rule kinds and date-governing source kinds as well as source/profile reads. Unrelated close-policy changes preserve card/journal approvals and mandates; item-effective review policy governs required model holds. Pure handlers receive declared source/profile inputs, committed dependency outputs and relevant dispositions. They cannot query storage, approve work, call a model or invoke external effects. The runner persists exact manifests, checkpoints, bounded leases and retry attempts. Dependency package controls propagate by default; a blocked inventory cannot yield an unblocked downstream package. Close readiness waits for earlier queued/running preparation in its period, then captures completed upstream outcomes when its execution begins; this refresh changes only that outcome dependency, never silently adopts revised sources/policy.

## Durable periods and source lifecycle

Periods are durable records with immutable status history. Lock/reopen requires a granted, independent human. Locked periods refuse dispatch and every review decision. Unsettled governing work requires an explicit recorded acknowledgement before lock. A lock preserves completed run references; it does not change historical accounting evidence.

Sources accept explicit timestamp or sequence as_of, tombstones and supersession. Older/conflicting versions remain history without replacing the accepted head. Legacy fictional arrivals explicitly use arrival order; batch names are not provider sequences. Superseded pending authorizations leave the current population with links retained. Approved-source revision/removal raises an accounting event. Replays use accepted head bytes and can re-evaluate unassigned bindings.

Dated sources route to their accounting period. Receipts follow the referenced transaction's period but retain their receipt date for policy matching. Unassigned evidence remains quarantined until its period opens. A move from a locked origin is quarantined with both period IDs; it enters no destination preparation. Reopening the origin re-evaluates the move and makes old preparation stale before the destination can govern. Historical versions and immutable artifact bytes are never rewritten.

## Accounting substance

Structured fictional statements declare balances, signed totals by activity, line count/identities and provenance. Workpapers disclose excluded/pending/foreign population; checks compare balance arithmetic, activity totals, identity and prior closing balance. The demonstrated statement cycle equals the configured period; other cycle-to-period policies require client validation. Legacy count/total controls alone cannot confer eligibility. The test-only reviewer adapter constructs explicit fictional specifications, never runtime statement evidence.

Shared proposed_entry records bind entity, book, period, currency, lines/dimensions, entry type, origin references and not_posted status. One common validator checks mechanical balance, account, line, scope, currency and period constraints. Current client dimension activation remains deferred. Card entries use an explicit fictional liability mapping. Overlapping origin claims hold both preparations. Journals additionally require journal-bound support, duplicate checks and a nonzero effect by account/dimension. None of this establishes correct recognition, tax treatment or actual ledger balances.

Exceptions have current status plus immutable history. Granted humans select evidence-bound item dispositions from a closed vocabulary. Re-preparation cites these decisions and still requires independent review. Split allocations must sum exactly. A proposed reusable rule does not resolve an item or activate policy. Revocation re-blocks preparation. Explicit refer_external dispositions retain their original pending/currency/missing-document blockers; they never make an item eligible.

Receipt matching uses approved parameters with explicit null values: date window, amount tolerance, multiple receipts, vendor method and statement substitution. Unset parameters retain exact matching and name the unset policy when a mismatch arises. No default tolerance is shipped.

## Content identity, independence and finalization

One completed governing artifact exists per workflow/period/item. Byte-identical work reuses its artifact and decisions across execution actors. Contributors are append-only provenance outside the content hash. Review uses the union of legacy envelope provenance and every linked-run contributor. Item lineage includes all historical suppliers of its evidence/duplicate inputs, applied rule proposers/approvers, disposition actors, enqueue actor, worker and mandate issuer. Supplying another item's receipt does not bar review of this item.

Decisions bind the exact artifact/hash/fingerprint and require a granted non-contributing human. Conflicts create disputed; explicit independent resolution is required. Mistaken revocation can be resolved by a new independent human. An overruled reviewer cannot repeatedly reopen a resolved judgment on unchanged preparation. All prior decisions remain immutable.

A granted independent human may finalize an immutable preparation package containing governing IDs/hashes/content, decisions, contributor sets, dispositions, manifest/rule versions, explicitly carried exceptions and the audit head. Disputed/stale items and package controls refuse finalization. Unreviewed or item-level carried work requires an explicit acknowledgement. This is preparation handover, not ledger posting or external close. Later input/decision changes raise POST_FINALIZATION_CHANGE; frozen package payload/hash remain byte-stable.

Close readiness evaluates typed workflow outcomes and period/account-bound evidence. A typed reconciliation/tieout requirement needs a verified workflow outcome; a document label cannot complete a deferred capability. It discloses stale, disputed, blocked, unreviewed and quarantined work. External verification and final close sign-off holds always remain; placeholder documents cannot prove an external reconciliation.

## Recurring work and limits

Mandates are proposed until a separately granted human approves them. They bind policy versions, explicit fixed/calendar/previous-month period selection, timestamp freshness predicates, expiry, occurrence caps and workflow scope. Missing fresh evidence leaves awaiting_evidence; intake can wake that occurrence on the persisted simulated clock. Changed policy suspends authority pending reconfirmation. A held run can release its occurrence for redispatch; one completed run per occurrence is enforced. No real scheduler is activated.

The local identity selector/grant fixture is a test harness, not authentication. Live connectors, models, posting, payments, production deployment and paid hosting are absent. Full export still uses two transactions with an input-change guard; atomic decision-consistent export and externally anchored audit are T-25, outside this batch. Worker failure classification/backoff/heartbeat, broader currency support and bounded production reads remain Batch 3. Finalized package bytes are immutable despite these live-export limitations. Schema migrations preserve historical records; tests cover concurrent migration, foreign keys, restart and immutable legacy bytes.

## RV correction details

The latest completed run now defines a workflow/period's governing population. Keys absent from that run have no governing artifact; their immutable artifacts/decisions, removal/move/supersession references and exception history remain inspectable under removed_items. Re-enqueue after a disposition is revoked creates a new execution projection when needed and reuses identical historical content without discarding its rejection. Unchanged snapshots are not rebound as late evidence merely because a period is locked.

Supersession links participate in the same locked-origin control as same-key revisions. A new key and its support remain quarantined while the superseded origin is locked; the old head is not removed until an authorized reopen permits admission. Envelope and data transitions both require review. Unknown predecessor intent is retained and held as unresolved, rather than silently treated as a clean charge. Proposed-entry ownership follows economic source identity across versions and supersession; noncurrent journal origin references remain held.

Profile approval retires withdrawn profile-derived mappings through immutable effective-dated retirement records. Unrelated and earlier-period rules remain in force for their dates. Applied rule lineage retains authors of earlier supersession versions whose matching/coding parameters are still consumed, while materially different earlier coding is excluded. Rule previews display normalized aliases and colliding rule IDs.

New close requirements must be typed objects. Existing untyped records are unsatisfied with UNTYPED_REQUIREMENT and are never silently promoted to reconciliation proof. Statement workpapers/items disclose line identity, prior continuity and supplying actor/provenance. Missing required prior statements hold the account; absent line IDs hold unless a separately approved statement policy explicitly permits them. Quarantined revisions of a locked prior statement cannot replace its admitted continuity evidence.

Disposition choices are constrained by transaction activity and active account type. Candidate sets bind duplicate decisions; new candidates invalidate them. Excluding a duplicate clears the surviving candidate truthfully, cites the decision and includes its author/support in lineage. Supporting evidence is required for both sides of a distinct-purchases decision. Corrected receipts/documents retain earlier references, changed fields and suppliers and produce EVIDENCE_REVISED_REQUIRES_REVIEW. A granted human who supplied no version must explicitly accept the revision; final review is still independent. Referrals retain their original blockers.

Mandates reject empty, unknown-kind or malformed freshness predicates. A no-evidence demonstration requires an explicit reason included in independent approval/audit; there is no silent default. Existing empty legacy mandates are suspended pending replacement authority. ## Card-feed contract and target selection (D-026)

The owner's feed contract D-026, with clarification D-029, governs fictional card transaction rows and their receipts. Journals are outside it, and whether a real provider behaves this way is a G2 confirmation. It is implemented in `accounting_agent/feed_contract.py` on the existing append-only accounting events, source links and dispositions; no schema changed. Its promise is that, for every delivery order the contract allows, one charge is never approved in a locked period and again in an open one, and nothing leaves a locked-period hold without a person's authority. It does not promise identical intermediate records in every order.

- **Provider order.** Where messages carry a sequence number or timestamp, the highest governs the row. An older or conflicting message is retained as a source version with an audit record and never replaces the governing row. One ordering scheme applies to a row (D-038): a message with no order value for a row governed by one, carrying different data, is kept once however often it repeats, never governs, and is flagged as SOURCE_ORDER_MIXED in the state, the export and the Source evidence view of the local page. The same data without an order value is an ordinary repeat and is not flagged.
- **Replacement claims.** A message may declare that its row replaces another. The first claim for a row is recorded (SOURCE_CLAIM_DECLARED) and applied wherever it arrives: on the governing version, on a newly retained older or conflicting version, or on a re-send of a message already retained. If the target is on file, the rows are linked and approvals along the chain raise approved-source events. If it is not, the claim waits (SOURCE_SUPERSESSION_UNRESOLVED), the row is blocked, and it links when the target arrives.
- **Repeats and omissions.** Repeating a claim the row already has changes no source lifecycle record. A later version that does not repeat a claim does not withdraw it. Under D-038 the same holds for an identical repeat, claim included, of a row or of its receipt that a live replacement, admitted to its period, has retired: no new version, no change, and the batch keeps its delivery record. A re-send with a newer order value, a re-send after the provider withdrew the replacement or the row itself, a re-send that omits a claim the row has, and a re-send while the replacement is itself held, for instance under a locked period, are recorded as before; in the last case the re-sent row takes the hold with its chain. A new batch keeps its own delivery record; an exact replay adds nothing.
- **Second claim naming a different target.** It is recorded (SOURCE_CLAIM_CONFLICT_RECORDED) and not applied. The row and its live replacements carry the SOURCE_CLAIM_CONFLICT blocker and any approval stops governing. The row is neither moved into a hold nor out of one, and the second target is not retired. Further different targets join the same open question.
- **Locked periods.** If a chain reaches a locked period, the row, its live replacements and their receipts are held under that period. A provider message alone never releases the hold. An approval already frozen in the row's own locked period is preserved and the incoming version is held until an authorized reopen; reopen does not clear a contested-claim blocker.
- **Target selection.** Only a recorded disposition clears the contested-claim blocker: choice `select_supersession_target` with payload `target_key`, through the ordinary disposition operation (service call or POST /api/dispositions) for a single card item in its accounting-date period. It requires the reviewer role with a disposition grant, a target among the row's declared targets, an open home period, current scoped supporting evidence related to the item, no cycle, and a person who supplied none of the row's claim messages. When the selection changes the standing target and would release a held charge from a locked period other than the new target's origin, the same person must also hold the lock grant. It records SOURCE_CLAIM_TARGET_SELECTED and approves nothing. Confirming the standing target moves nothing. A selection is not revoked through the ordinary revocation route; a new equally authorized selection replaces it, and new different claims reopen the question.
- **Review independence.** Every person who recorded a target selection for a row, or for any row it was ever linked to, stays a contributor of the row, its revisions and its live replacements, and so cannot review or finalize them. A selector is also a contributor of that card run's period-level artifacts: the statement workpaper, the inventory and the package manifest. The owner accepted this stricter scope in D-038.
- **Report cost.** Within one database transaction and one report calculation, a run's currentness is computed once; outside a transaction every artifact is evaluated afresh.

The local web page has no control for target selection. The owner decided UC-21 to UC-23 in D-038; UC-24 is carried to upgrade notes and UC-25 needs no action. U4, UC-14, UC-17, UC-18 and UC-20 remain open. The independent RE review accepted RE-02 and RE-03; the independent RF review accepted the rest and verified the promise above in 3,120 stepwise delivery orders. That is evidence for the orders tried on fictional data, not proof for every provider or client workflow. The D-038 changes were made after that review. They are covered by review tests Fable built (tests/test_feed_repeat_decisions.py) and by re-running the reviewers' unmodified probes, searches and gates, not by a separate review.
