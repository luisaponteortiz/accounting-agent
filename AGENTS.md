## Standing project rules

The user's project scope supersedes the broader Supporting Strategies reference handoff. Build a reusable general accounting execution platform covering GL, AP, AR, banking/reconciliation, month-end close and financial reporting/analysis. Bird Bowl is the first client implementation; card preparation is one demonstration, not the product boundary. Do not implement executive, marketing, sales or other non-accounting agents here.

The platform must remain universal across clients. Keep client/industry/jurisdiction-specific accounting knowledge and system choices in isolated profiles, not hard-coded Bird Bowl assumptions in the shared core. New clients require onboarding and capability validation; a reusable architecture is not a claim that every client or treatment is already supported.

Before implementation, read `docs/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md`, the authoritative product requirements register, along with the relevant existing backlog tickets, `DECISIONS.md`, `PROGRESS.md` and current corrective gates. Reference the applicable stable requirement IDs in implementation decisions. Whenever a feature is added, modified, completed, deferred or rejected, update the register's description, dependencies, status, acceptance criteria, target stage and evidence as applicable, and synchronize decisions/progress in the same change. Preserve IDs and retain rejected/superseded requirements with their reason and decision; never silently drop future scope. Record unassigned requirements without inserting them into an authorized batch. Product approval does not grant client-policy, integration, deployment, spending, communication, payment or posting authority. Preserve Fable's exact assertions, corrective priorities, original evidence and frozen bundles.

Keep Product decision separate from Implementation state. Implemented or higher states require a named scope note and evidence pointer; Developer-tested requires named tests/probes with recorded results. Only a recorded review may establish Independently verified for the specified source/scope: review tests or probes that are separate from the work's own unit tests, are shown able to fail, run on an identified checkpoint, and have a results file and report pinned in the checker. By owner decision D-037 the reviewer may be Fable (Claude) reviewing work Fable itself created, and the record's evidence must then say that the same author built the work and its review tests. Developer tests alone cannot establish it. A failed independent probe must downgrade every affected record to Verification failed in the same change; passing developer tests cannot erase that verdict. Re-verification must identify its checkpoint and exact criteria. Run the repeatable register checker through `make check` after every register edit. It must reject duplicate IDs, missing fields, unknown/cyclic dependencies, missing backlog crosswalk tickets and invalid status/evidence values. Record conflicts explicitly rather than choosing a weaker control. Each additional client's facts belong in that client's isolated configuration/knowledge files or clearly labeled client section, never in shared requirement criteria.

Keep shared controls/workflow tools separate from client policies, chart, vendors/customers, systems, decisions, procedures and credentials. Enforce organization/client/environment scope in backend reads, jobs, evidence, approvals and exports. Unknown Bird Bowl facts stay unknown; all current accounts, mappings, financial data and review identities are fictional.

Development builds use fictional or sandbox data only and, under D-040, may be hosted and may call model services with keys and a spending ceiling the owner provides. Do not connect a client's real systems, load real client records, post entries, send messages or execute payments without the owner's specific go-live authorization. Do not promote synthetic profiles or approvals into real operation. No model proposal may grant authority or activate policy.

Preserve the original `ss_agent_system_handoff/` package. Maintain `PROGRESS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `docs/CONNECTION_READINESS.md` and the Fable handoff. Record honest capability states; no full-cycle, production or human operating-readiness claim from fixtures. Run `make test` and relevant checks after code changes. Local HTTP tests need loopback-port access. Do not add unrelated toolchains just to claim a standard stack.
